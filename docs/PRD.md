# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Product Name

NasFon Store

## Version

1.0 (MVP)

## Document Owner

NasFon Team

---

# 1. Product Overview

NasFon Store is an e-commerce web application designed specifically for people who are afraid of shopping online. Instead of focusing only on selling products, the platform focuses on building trust, reducing uncertainty, and making first-time online shopping simple and safe.

The MVP will initially sell genuine phone accessories while creating an experience that gives customers confidence at every step—from browsing products to receiving their orders. For now, the business will focus on one city first, and only admins can create and manage delivery addresses.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Many Nigerians avoid online shopping because they fear:

* Online scams
* Fake products
* Losing their money
* Complicated checkout processes
* Poor customer support
* No updates after payment
* Unreliable delivery

Existing marketplaces often assume customers already trust online shopping.

NasFon Store is designed to earn that trust.

---

# 3. Vision

To become Nigeria's most trusted online store for people who are afraid to shop online, helping first-time buyers feel safe, confident, and comfortable while shopping for genuine items.

---

# 4. Mission

Provide a secure, transparent, and easy shopping experience where customers know exactly what they are buying, how much they are paying, and what happens after placing an order.

---

# 5. Goals

## Business Goals

# 10. Functional Requirements

Customers can:

* Browse products.
* Search products.
* View detailed product information.
* Add products to cart.
* Checkout securely without creating an account.
* Create an account optionally for order tracking and faster future checkout.
* Sign in securely if they already have an account.
* Pay online.
* Track orders.
* View order history.
* Leave reviews after delivery.
* Select from available delivery locations created by admin.

Sellers can (after verification):

* Manage own products.
* Manage own inventory.
* Upload product images.
* View orders containing own products (read-only).
* View sales dashboard.

Admins can:

* Manage products (all, including seller products).
* Manage categories.
* Manage inventory.
* Create and manage delivery addresses.
* Process orders.
* Update order status.
* View analytics.
* Manage customers.
* Manage sellers (approve, reject, suspend, view documents).
